Caring For Your Coating

A ceramic coating is durable, but it isn't bulletproof. Three things will damage it:

  • Automatic and tunnel car washes. The spinning brushes drag grit across the panel and can scratch straight through the coating and into your clear coat.
  • Clay barring a coated surface. Clay is an abrasive — running it over a finished coating strips the coating off.
  • Strong acidic cleaners. Aggressive wheel and paint chemicals break the coating down early.

Hand wash or touchless wash only once the coating is on. Follow that and you'll get the full rated life out of it.
