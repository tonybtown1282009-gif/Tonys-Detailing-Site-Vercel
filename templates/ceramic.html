What It Is

Protection That Bonds To Your Paint

A ceramic coating is a liquid protective layer, applied by hand, that bonds to your vehicle's paint. Once it cures it adds depth and gloss and makes the vehicle easier to keep clean between washes.

Unlike the wax protection coat in a regular detail, a coating is rated in years. Our 3-Year package uses a 3-year rated coating; the Elite package uses DIY Detail's 5-year ceramic coating, rated for 5+ years with proper maintenance.

Why Proper Prep Matters

A coating locks in whatever condition the paint is in. Every step below is done by hand before the coating goes on, so it bonds to properly prepped, polished paint rather than to whatever is sitting on top of it.

Long-Term Protection

Caring For Your Coating

A ceramic coating is durable, but it isn't bulletproof. Hand wash or touchless wash only once the coating is on — follow that and you'll get the full rated life out of it.

Three Things That Damage A Coating

  • {{ icon('circle-alert') }}Automatic and tunnel car washes. The spinning brushes drag grit across the panel and can scratch through the coating into your clear coat.
  • {{ icon('circle-alert') }}Clay barring a coated surface. Clay is an abrasive — running it over a finished coating strips it off.
  • {{ icon('circle-alert') }}Strong acidic cleaners. Aggressive wheel and paint chemicals break the coating down early.
