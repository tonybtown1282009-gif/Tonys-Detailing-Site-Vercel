About Us

Meet Tony

Tony's Detailing started with a simple idea: do fewer cars, but do them right. Every booking is handled personally by Tony — from the first walk-around to the final wipe-down — so the quality never changes hands.

We come to you — your home or office. You don't rearrange your day or drop your car at a shop; we set up in your driveway and get to work. All we ask is access to an outlet and, in some cases, an outdoor water spigot.

We're upfront about what a detail can and can't do, we confirm your quote before we start, and we take before-and-after photos on every job — for your records and ours. It's detailing you can trust, from someone who stakes his name on it.
